Excel to PDF Converter

Convert Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files to PDF free. Runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

Each sheet becomes a table in the PDF. Standard Latin characters work best.

How it works

  1. Choose your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Tap Convert to PDF.
  3. Check the preview and download your file.

Frequently asked questions

Is my spreadsheet uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so your file never leaves your device.

Are all sheets converted?

Yes. Each non-empty sheet starts on its own page. Very large sheets are limited to the first 2,000 rows.

Do formulas and colors carry over?

The PDF shows calculated values in a clean table. Cell colors and charts are not copied.
