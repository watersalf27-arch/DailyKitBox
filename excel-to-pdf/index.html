Excel to Image Converter

Convert an Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV sheet to a PNG image free. Private, runs in your browser.

The first sheet is rendered as a PNG image (up to 300 rows).

How it works

  1. Choose your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Tap Convert to PNG image.
  3. Check the preview and download your file.

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet becomes the image?

The first non-empty sheet. Move the sheet you want to the front in Excel if needed.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is drawn in your browser.

Why are long cell values shortened?

Columns are capped at a readable width, so very long text ends with an ellipsis.
