Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

DailyKitBox's tools — including Merge PDF, Split PDF, Delete PDF Pages, Images to PDF, the Office Converter and every calculator on this site — run entirely in your browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. This policy explains what limited information we do collect and why.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not receive, store, view or transmit the documents, images or files you upload into any DailyKitBox tool. Merging, splitting, converting, watermarking and every other file operation happens locally using your browser's own processing power. Once you close or refresh the tab, that data is gone from your device's memory — we never had a copy in the first place.

2. Information We Do Collect

Like most websites, we automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Approximate location, device type and browser (via analytics)
  • Pages visited, time on page and referring website
  • IP address, used only in aggregate for security and analytics

If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and to improve our tools.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use cookies to remember your preferences (such as dark or light mode), to understand how visitors use our tools, and to serve advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads on this site based on a user's past visits.

4. Analytics

We may use web analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns. These services may set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyKitBox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain data processing. Since DailyKitBox's tools never receive your files, this mainly applies to the limited analytics and cookie data described above. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

Because file processing happens entirely in your browser, your documents are never exposed to transmission or server-side risk. For the limited data we do collect, we use standard industry safeguards to protect it from unauthorized access.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of DailyKitBox after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@dailykitbox.com or visit our Contact page.
