Excel to Word Converter

Convert Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files to an editable Word (.docx) table free. Private, runs in your browser.

Each sheet becomes a table in a landscape Word document.

How it works

  1. Choose your Excel or CSV file.
  2. Tap Convert to Word.
  3. Check the preview and download your file.

Frequently asked questions

Is my spreadsheet uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. The output is a normal .docx file with real tables you can edit in Word or Google Docs.

Are charts and colors included?

No. Only cell values are converted. Very large sheets are limited to the first 2,000 rows.
